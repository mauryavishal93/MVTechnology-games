About this policy

MV-Labs Technology (“we,” “us,” or “our”) creates games, mobile and web applications, and other software projects. This policy describes how information may be handled when you visit our company website or use our products, including Find My Puppy, Tidal Warfare, Dots and Boxes, Brain Peace: Scroll Tracker, and Build My Portfolio.

Our products can have different features and use different service providers. This website does not confirm that every product collects the same information or uses the same technologies. Where a product provides an additional privacy notice, that notice explains its specific practices and should be read together with this policy.

Information you provide

If you email us or otherwise contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name, and message, so we can respond and handle your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information by email.

We do not operate an account or payment form on this company website. If you use a feature in one of our apps that asks you to provide information, the app’s own notice will explain what is requested and why.

Technical information and cookies

When this website is hosted or accessed, the hosting provider and standard internet infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, and access times for delivery, reliability, and security. The particular information retained depends on the hosting provider and its settings.

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies through its own page code. Third-party websites and services linked from our pages may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Games, apps, and service providers

Our games and apps may rely on platform, hosting, or other third-party services to operate. Those providers may process information as described in their own privacy notices and in the disclosures shown in the relevant app or app-store listing. The information and permissions involved can vary by product and feature.

For the most current details about a particular mobile app—including any data collection, sharing, permissions, or deletion options—please review that app’s privacy information on its store listing and within the app, where available. Google Play and other external sites are operated by their respective providers; we do not control their privacy practices.

How we use information

Information sent directly to us is used to reply to you, provide requested support, and maintain our communications. Technical information handled by our hosting provider may be used to deliver and secure the website. We do not use information submitted through this website to build advertising profiles.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information submitted directly to us. We may share a message with a service provider when needed to receive or respond to it, or disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or services. Information is kept only for as long as needed for the purpose it was received, to resolve a request, or to meet legal obligations.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose not to contact us or provide optional information. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your request before responding.

Children’s privacy

Our products may differ in their intended audience and features. We do not knowingly ask children to send personal information to us through this company website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us so we can review the request. Check the relevant app’s store listing and in-app notice for age-related information about that product.

Security and changes

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We may update this policy as our website, apps, or practices change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when this page was most recently revised.
